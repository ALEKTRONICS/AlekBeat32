DrumProject — Flash your ESP32

Use the button to connect to your ESP32 and flash the latest firmware and filesystem image (LittleFS). Works in Chromium-based browsers with WebUSB/WebSerial support.

View manifest Back to project
Tip: Put your board in bootloader/flash mode, allow the browser to access the port, and avoid other programs holding the COM port open.
Need help? Open the browser console for messages while flashing.
